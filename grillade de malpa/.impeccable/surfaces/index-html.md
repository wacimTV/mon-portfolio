Scope: index.html, the whole one-page site. Mode: Persuade.
Audience: everyone in the quartier and passers-by, mostly on phones; job: decide to come and book a table.

## Direction contract

THESIS: The site IS the counter's letter board: black ribbed felt, white push-in plastic letters, a wooden frame. It refuses the cream-and-serif bistro page and the dark smoky BBQ hero.

OWN-WORLD: Felt black #141312 with horizontal ribs every 8px; letters off-white #efeae0 in a condensed grotesque (Archivo Narrow 700, uppercase, wide tracking), body in Archivo; wood frame #8a6a43. Ember orange #ff5a1f is the only warm colour: prices and the Réserver action only (raise from nixie counter). Every line of letters sits on a rib, nothing off the grid (raise from arcade). No decorative shadows: depth only by stacking frame, felt, letters, photos (raise from papercut).

STORY: The visitor reads the board like at the counter: the name, what is on the fire, the prices, where and when. They believe the meat is cooked on charcoal. They pick a day, an hour and how many, and book.

FIRST VIEWPORT: A framed board fills the screen. Left two thirds: LA GRILLADE DE MALPA set large in letters, the line AU CHARBON DE BOIS · MALPASSÉ 13E, three price rows. Right third: a photo of the embers set into the board. Bottom rail of the board: date, hour and party-size selects as letter slots plus the orange RÉSERVER, which carries the choice into the full form.

FORM: Le tableau du comptoir, position 4 on my list; seed:3b9c6349d1a12b36. Signature interaction: on load the letters are pushed into the board line by line, like someone composing the board; under reduced motion they are simply there.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
